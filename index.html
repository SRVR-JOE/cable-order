Cable Order — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Cable Order is a stock-room tool. Technicians scan the label on a cable bin, enter a quantity, and send the resulting order list by email or the iOS Share sheet. This policy explains what happens to information in the app.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of its own. It does not send order data to us or to any server we operate.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage:

  • The name, recipient email address and CC address you type in Setup.
  • The order you are building, a short history of orders you have sent, and any catalog edits.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to send an order. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR or barcode label on a bin. Images are processed on the device in real time and are not saved or uploaded.

Sending an order

When you tap Send or Share, the app hands the order text to Apple Mail or to the app you pick in the iOS Share sheet (for example Messages or Microsoft Teams). That message is then handled by that app and its provider under their own privacy policies. Cable Order does not see or keep a copy of it after hand-off.

Third-party components

Barcode reading uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the device. Google's ML Kit SDK may send limited, anonymous diagnostic and performance information to Google to keep the component working. This information is not linked to you and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Google ML Kit terms.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
